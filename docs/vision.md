# Remin — Vision

> Understand your life. Improve it deliberately.

Remin is a local-first personal intelligence system that helps its owner understand, organize, and improve life through structured memory, thoughtful automation, and trustworthy AI.

Remin is not an AI chatbot.

Remin is not a productivity app.

Remin is not a wardrobe manager.

Those are applications of a much larger system.

## What Remin Exists To Do

Remin exists to reduce cognitive load.

Instead of remembering everything yourself, Remin maintains structured knowledge about your possessions, routines, projects, preferences, goals, and decisions so that AI can reason from facts rather than assumptions.

## Long-Term Vision

A system capable of:

- Knowing the state of everything you intentionally choose to track.
- Learning carefully from repeated human feedback.
- Providing recommendations without taking away agency.
- Coordinating specialized modules through clearly defined responsibilities.
- Remaining useful regardless of which AI models exist in the future.

## Success Criteria

If Remin succeeds:

- The user remembers less while understanding more.
- Decisions become easier, not automated.
- AI becomes replaceable.
- Data remains owned by the user.
- The system grows alongside its owner for years rather than months.
